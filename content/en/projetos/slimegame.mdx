Slimegame is a 2D metroidvania I'm making in Godot, inspired by Hollow Knight, Silksong and Blasphemous, with souls-like combat.

The story is still in development, but for now the idea is this: you play as Gota, a slime that became conscious after absorbing the fragment of a fallen god. She explores two connected worlds, the surface and the underground, and progresses by stealing her enemies' abilities.

## What's in the game so far

Gota has three forms, Slime, Human and Knight, and each one jumps, deals with walls and water and attacks in its own way. Combat has timing-based combos, a guard that wears down, parries and red attacks that can't be blocked, plus a short hit-stop and camera shake on heavy hits.

During fights the Blight grows, an infestation that makes attacks stronger but comes at a price, and that can be purged with an area attack. There are already several enemies, like bats, golems, grobs, wolves and spiders, each with its own behavior. The world has room transitions, checkpoints, hazard zones and saved progress, and when Gota dies her coins stay where she fell.

## How I make it

I draw all the art by hand in Aseprite. In the code, each part of the character, like movement, combat, abilities and forms, is a separate module, which lets me change one without breaking the others. The core mechanics have automated tests, and I keep a game design document updated with every change.
