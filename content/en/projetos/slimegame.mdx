## The game

You play as **Gota**, a slime that gained consciousness after absorbing a Fragment of a fallen god. She explores two connected worlds, **Alterra** (the surface) and **the Depths** (the underground abyss), and progress comes from abilities stolen from enemies, not from keys. The inspirations are Hollow Knight, Silksong and Blasphemous, with souls-like combat.

## How it works

- **Component-based player:** movement, dash, combat, visuals, collision, abilities and forms are separate modules sharing a single state.
- **Forms:** Slime, Human and Knight, each with its own jump, its own relationship with walls and water, and its own attack.
- **Combat:** combos with per-hit timing and hitboxes, a guard that wears down, a 0.3 s parry, red attacks that can't be blocked, hit-stop and camera shake on heavy hits.
- **Blight:** an infestation that grows during encounters, strengthens your attacks and comes at a price; you can purge it in a single area attack.
- **Enemies:** bat, golem (light, heavy and flying), grob, wolf, spider and others, each with its own AI.
- **World and saving:** room transitions, checkpoints, hazard zones, currency that stays where you died, and saved progress.

The art is hand-drawn in Aseprite, and a Python script builds the HUD sprite sheets from the source files.
