*In progress: an internship project, built in a pair.*

Tremor is one of the symptoms that most gets in the way for people with Parkinson's, in simple things like eating, writing and getting dressed. The main medication loses its effect over the years, and the surgery used in some cases is expensive and invasive. Steadyhand aims to be an affordable option that you just put on.

## How it will work

1. **Sensor:** the glove follows the hand's movement all the time.
2. **Vibration:** when the movement has the rhythm of a tremor, it recognizes that it isn't a normal gesture.
3. **FES:** it applies mild electrical stimulation to the muscles, known as functional electrical stimulation, which helps hold the tremor back.

The glove only acts when the tremor shows up. The rest of the time, it stays out of the way.

## Where it stands

The project is still at the start. We've done the planning and studied Parkinson's and the similar gloves that already exist. The next step is collecting the first data with the sensor.
